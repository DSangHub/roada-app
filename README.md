# Roada.app

Savings membership app for high-mileage professionals (truckers, contractors, plumbers, work-truck drivers doing 100+ miles/day).

## Key Features
- Digital barcode/QR membership card
- Mileage tracker (on/off + always-on option)
- Food receipt barcode reader + in-app store function
- Membership levels: Silver (2,000 miles/month), Gold (5,000 miles/month)
- $500/month food spend bonus
- Points for prizes (sample prizes: iPhone, Roada leather jacket, boots, hat)
- Discounts at participating gas stations and fast food (partners display welcome signs)
- Spanish toggle
- Start in the US, then expand to Canada & Mexico
- Backend for profiles
- Major credit card issuer partnership for extra cash back
